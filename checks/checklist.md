# Human Review Checklist

Go through this before you decide how to respond.

- [ ] The diagnosis is one of the five states, not a vague "they need more time"
- [ ] Fact is kept apart from inference: what was said versus what's read from tone or timing
- [ ] A soft no or disqualification is named plainly, not softened to stay hopeful
- [ ] Nothing invents a concession, incentive or promise to make a decision feel safer or faster
- [ ] If the evidence was too thin to diagnose, it said so rather than guessing
