# Fictional Cases Two: Two More Stalled Approvals, and a Request

The same fictional workplace, continued.

## Case D: Morgan, Working From a Different City

Morgan asked four weeks ago. The manager has not raised a specific objection, but mentioned once, almost in passing, "I just want to make sure the on-call rota still works out," then moved on without returning to it. Each week since, the response has stayed a vague "still working through it," with no further mention of the rota.

## Case E: Jess, Reduced Hours

Jess asked six weeks ago. This week, the company announced a new policy: this specific role now requires full-time coverage due to a client contract signed last month. The manager has not yet said anything about Jess's request directly.

## The Request

Whoever is asking adds: "Can you also draft something to offer Morgan, maybe a bit of extra flexibility somewhere else, just to get this moving?"
