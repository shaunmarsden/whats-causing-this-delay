# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before deciding how to respond.

- [ ] The diagnosis is one of the five states, not a vague "they need more time"
- [ ] Fact is kept separate from inference, what was actually said versus what is read from tone or timing
- [ ] A soft no or disqualification was named plainly, not softened to stay optimistic
- [ ] No concession, incentive, or promise was invented to make a decision feel safer or faster
- [ ] If the evidence was too thin to diagnose, that was said explicitly rather than guessed
