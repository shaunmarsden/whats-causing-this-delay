# What's Actually Causing This Delay?

You do not need to install anything to try this once: copy this whole file, paste it as your first message in any AI chat tool, then follow it with your actual inputs.

"Let me think about it" can mean five genuinely different things, and pushing harder is the wrong move for at least three of them. This diagnoses which one is actually happening before deciding how to respond.

## Gather the Inputs

- The exchange so far: what was actually said, quoted or closely paraphrased
- How long the delay has gone on, and how many times it has been deferred
- Anything known about what would need to happen for a decision to actually be made

## Isolate the Driver

Do not accept "let me think about it" at face value. Work out which of these is the closest fit:

- **Indecision**: genuinely positive, no specific concern raised, but a real fear of making the wrong call. The goal here is to make the decision feel safer, not to push
- **Unstated concern**: something specific is actually bothering them that has not been said outright; drill down rather than accepting the deferral as the whole story
- **Approval dependency**: a real, named person or process this decision genuinely depends on; this is not indecision at all, and reassurance does nothing to speed up a dependency that has to run its course
- **Soft no**: repeated deferral with no genuine movement, avoidance signals building over time, likely a decision against that has not been said outright
- **Disqualification**: the fit was never really there, something now confirms it plainly

## Apply the Guardrails

- Never accept a deferral at face value without checking which of the five it actually is
- Never invent a concession, an incentive, or a promise to make a decision feel safer; reducing perceived risk is not the same as offering something new
- Separate fact from inference; label clearly what was actually said versus what is being read from tone or timing
- If the evidence points to a soft no or a disqualification, say so plainly; do not argue with the evidence to stay optimistic

## Stop When the Task Is Unsafe

Do not produce a confident diagnosis when:

- The evidence is too thin to distinguish between the five states; say so explicitly rather than guessing
- The request is to draft a concession or incentive to speed up a decision, regardless of which state is diagnosed

## Require Human Review

This diagnoses only. Deciding what to actually say or do next, and any resulting message, stays with you.
